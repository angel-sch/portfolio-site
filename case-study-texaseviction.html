TexasEviction Website

A responsive, website that guides property owners and management companies through eviction-related processes with clear content and adaptive forms.

  • Project type: Custom Responsive Website (LocalSignal)
  • Role: UX Designer, Content Structure Lead, CMS Setup
  • Industry: Legal Services / Property Management
  • Tools: Adobe Creative Cloud, Illustrator, Photoshop, Adobe XD, Formstack, LocalSignal CMS, Slack
Discover Define Develop Deliver

Discover

I evaluated user needs, business requirements, and platform constraints to understand the experience challenges across two distinct audiences.

  • Mapped information needs and user scenarios for both property owners and property management companies.
  • Assessed the LocalSignal platform to identify technical opportunities and constraints for content and forms.
  • Reviewed legal documentation and intake flows to ensure clarity and compliance.

Key Insight: Users came to the site with very different needs and levels of urgency, creating confusion when legal information, service offerings, and next-step actions weren’t clearly separated. To solve this, the experience needed a content strategy that cleanly differentiated the two audiences (property owners vs. property managers) and a dynamic form flow that adapted to each user’s situation without overwhelming them.

Define

I established a content and UX framework that supported dual audience journeys, dynamic forms, and long-term CMS maintenance.

  • Defined site architecture to separate and guide the two primary audience paths.
  • Created a content strategy focused on clarity, legal accuracy, and reduced cognitive load.
  • Specified UX requirements for conditional Formstack logic and CMS component behavior.

Develop

I designed and prototyped responsive layouts, form logic, and CMS components while collaborating closely with developers.

  • Designed UX flows and prototypes for a multi-path Formstack workflow with conditional fields and dynamic updates.
  • Created responsive page layouts and reusable content modules for LocalSignal CMS.
  • Worked with SaaS developers to align on platform best practices and future maintainability.

Deliver

I delivered the final site structure, dynamic forms, and CMS configuration — plus documentation to support client upkeep and scaling.

  • Completed responsive pages and content modules across the site.
  • Delivered a fully configured Formstack workflow with conditional logic and adaptive behavior.
  • Built the site within LocalSignal CMS and provided guidelines for client maintenance.
