Sporcle Live Trivia Collateral

Branding collateral for Sporcle Live's nationwide bar and pub trivia events in the United States.

  • Project type: Branding
  • Role: Product Designer
  • Industry: Entertainment / Media
  • Tools: Adobe Creative Cloud, Illustrator, Photoshop, Slack, Trello
Discover Define Develop Deliver

Discover

I assessed Sporcle's digital brand and the environments where live trivia took place to identify visibility and readability needs.

  • Reviewed Sporcle's existing digital brand to understand tone, personality, and visual guardrails.
  • Studied real bar and pub environments, focusing on low light, busy tabletops, and quick-glance interactions.
  • Evaluated how materials would be viewed — on tables, across the room, or held in hand — to understand real-world use.

Key Insight: The core brand color performed well, but printed materials needed enhanced contrast and clarity to remain readable in dim bar environments.

Define

I identified the core challenge and established the design criteria needed to extend Sporcle's brand into physical spaces.

  • The challenge: extend Sporcle's familiar digital identity into physical spaces while ensuring readability, visibility, and brand cohesion.
  • Identified opportunities to create a flexible system that works across various formats — from small coasters to large banners.
  • Established design criteria: high contrast, bold typography, simple iconography, and layouts optimized for quick scanning.

Develop

I explored visual directions, tested variations, and refined designs to ensure they worked in low-light, real-world settings.

  • Explored visual directions that balanced Sporcle's playful trivia tone with real-world practicality.
  • Created layout variations for multiple sizes and use cases (tabletop, wall-mounted, handheld).
  • Adjusted color and type treatments for low-light environments, testing visibility and readability.
  • Collaborated with team stakeholders to refine messaging hierarchy and adapt brand elements without losing recognition.

Deliver

I delivered a complete promotional ecosystem designed to bring Sporcle Live to life.

The finalized full promotional package included:

  • Coasters
  • Letter-sized flyers
  • Tri-fold table tents
  • Promotional inserts
  • Vinyl banners
  • Design system documentation
  • Design handoff and launch support

I developed print-ready files and digital assets optimized for multiple vendors and formats. Finally, I created guidelines to ensure consistent application of the extended brand across future materials.
