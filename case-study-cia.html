Construction Intern Awards Website

Updated and managed the Construction Intern Awards website to highlight scholarship winners and increase engagement year over year.

  • Project type: New Build / Ongoing Website Management
  • Role: UX Designer, Content Strategist, Analytics
  • Industry: Construction / Education
  • Tools: Adobe Creative Cloud, Adobe XD, Google Analytics, OpenWater CMS, Trello
Discover Define Develop Deliver

Discover

I researched user needs, campaign flows, and content update requirements to manage weekly scholarship highlights effectively.

  • Studied the audience: students, universities, and construction industry professionals.
  • Analyzed past website engagement to identify areas for improvement in content discoverability and highlight visibility.
  • Reviewed campaign timing and social media integration to ensure consistent promotion of scholarship winners.

Key Insight: Frequent updates and campaign promotions required a system that balanced rapid content changes with clear, engaging UX for both website and social media.

Define

I established content and UX workflows to manage weekly scholarship highlights and maximize engagement.

  • Defined a content update strategy for weekly scholarship winner features.
  • Designed information hierarchy and navigation for fast access to student profiles, application info, and award details.
  • Coordinated with social media campaigns to align messaging and timing.

Develop

Designed and implemented updates to the website, including mobile-friendly layouts, landing pages, and analytics tracking.

  • Created high-fidelity layouts in Adobe XD for award winner features and landing pages.
  • Implemented updates in OpenWater, ensuring seamless publishing of new scholarship profiles.
  • Monitored engagement using Google Analytics and iterated on UX to improve visibility and user interaction.
  • Coordinated with universities and internal teams to schedule content updates and campaigns efficiently.

Deliver

Delivered a mobile-friendly, user-focused site that effectively highlighted scholarship winners, supported campaigns, and doubled engagement year over year.

  • Launched updated website features for student profiles and award highlights.
  • Provided ongoing content updates, campaign integration, and analytics tracking.
  • Delivered a streamlined workflow for weekly updates, social promotion, and engagement measurement.
