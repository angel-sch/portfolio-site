Jumpix Branding

Created the Jumpix Branding Manual to establish a bold, playful identity with clear logo and color guidelines for consistent internal and external application.

  • Project type: Branding & Visual Identity Documentation
  • Role: UX/Visual Designer, Brand Strategist
  • Industry: SaaS / Enterprise Software
  • Tools: Adobe Creative Cloud, Adobe XD, InDesign, Photoshop, Illustrator, Slack, Trello
Discover Define Develop Deliver

Discover

Conducted research and analysis to understand market positioning and competitive branding in the enterprise software space.

  • Reviewed competitors in file migration and enterprise software.
  • Interviewed internal teams to understand company culture, values, and desired perception.
  • Explored how the “Jump” metaphor and frog logo could be applied creatively across visuals.

Key Insight: A playful, vibrant identity with bright colors and a distinctive frog logo would help Jumpix stand out in a traditionally corporate, subdued space.

Define

Established brand identity guidelines, naming rationale, and visual principles for consistent application.

  • Defined color palette, typography, and logo usage rules.
  • Established bold, playful colors and a distinctive frog logo to stand out in the enterprise file migration space.
  • Created design principles emphasizing boldness, clarity, and approachability.
  • Determined guidelines for internal and external facing collateral and brand communication tone.

Develop

Designed templates, visuals, and layout systems for print and digital materials.

  • Created InDesign layouts and templates for documentation, marketing, and collateral.
  • Designed logo variations, iconography, and visual assets in Illustrator and Photoshop.
  • Tested layouts and color combinations for legibility and brand recognition.

Deliver

Produced a comprehensive branding manual ensuring consistent application across all touchpoints.

  • Delivered finalized manual with rules for logo usage, color, typography, and collateral.
  • Provided guidelines for marketing materials, internal communications, and external documents.
  • Enabled consistent implementation of a disruptive, visually consistent brand.
